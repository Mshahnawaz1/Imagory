### New

- **Clean up first, from Add source.** When you add a Google Takeout folder, Imagory now offers **Clean up first** (the default) or **Add as is**. Clean up first opens Clean up Takeout with the folder already picked, and when the copy is done it adds the tidy copy to your library under the name you gave it, with your Google Photos albums. It needs about as much free space as the export. **Add as is** scans the export itself, as before, but Takeout albums only come in through a cleanup.

### Fixes

- **Removing a source now removes the albums it empties.** Before, the albums stayed in the sidebar with no photos in them, and adding a cleaned Takeout folder again created `<name> (Takeout)` copies of every album. The confirmation now names the albums that will go. Albums that were already empty are kept, and **Remove missing…** doesn't delete albums.
- **HEIC licence.** HEIC photos are now decoded with pi-heif, which only reads HEIC and is LGPL. 0.2.0 bundled pillow-heif, whose x265 encoder is GPLv2. Imagory is now MIT throughout, with its bundled components under their own permissive or LGPL licences.

### Known limits

- **Not code-signed.** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See [Windows warnings](/docs/windows-warnings).
- Takeout zips must be extracted first.
- Linux: on some distros Qt needs `libxcb-cursor0`; Show in folder opens the folder without selecting the photo.
